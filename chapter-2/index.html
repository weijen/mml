CHAPTER 02 / A VISUAL READING COMPANION

Linear algebra,
made visible.

From solving a pair of equations to seeing what a matrix really does. Build the intuition, follow the mathematics, then try it yourself.

Start gently →Go to practice

One chapter. One connected story.

The central question is “What can we make by adding vectors and multiplying them by numbers?” Equations ask for the right coefficients. Independence asks whether those coefficients are unique. A basis gives every vector an address. A linear map moves the vectors, while a basis change only changes their addresses.

Constraints→Combinations→Spaces & bases→Maps & coordinates

About this companion

Based on a reading of all of Chapter 2 (printed pp. 17–69, including Exercises 2.1–2.20) in your local Mathematics for Machine Learning PDF by Marc Peter Deisenroth, A. Aldo Faisal, and Cheng Soon Ong, draft dated January 15, 2024. The explanations and diagrams are written for this companion; selected small examples are identified where they follow the book.

PDF page numbers are six higher than printed numbers in this file, so printed p. 17 is PDF page 23. Every lesson links to its starting page. All diagrams, typography, and interactions work offline, with no external libraries. Progress is stored in this browser when local storage is available; some browsers restrict persistence for local files. The lessons and expandable explanations remain readable without JavaScript.

The illustrations use ordinary 2D coordinates for intuition. Lengths and angles are not the focus until Chapter 3. The practice guide includes hints and selected results, rather than a complete solutions manual.
